Theme background ideas — examples only

Your player is unchanged (still 5.8). Each panel shows a fake player card over the animated background, so you can judge how it feels behind the real UI. All four are pure CSS motion — cheap transforms, no audio routing, nothing that can touch lock-screen playback. In a real build the background would follow the theme automatically, like the dancer does.

Green Hill Groove
Sonic theme
⏮▶⏭

Sonic — rings

Gold rings doing the classic flip-spin while they bounce, each on its own timing so it never looks mechanical. Bright and playful. Could speed the spin up on the beat later, but simple looping already reads “Sonic”.

Chaos Control
Shadow theme
⏮▶⏭

Shadow — rings, his way

Same rings, Shadow’s mood: darker gold with a red glow, slower spin, lazier bounce. Same family as Sonic, completely different attitude.

Upload Complete
SoundCloud theme
⏮▶⏭

SoundCloud — drifting cloud

Soft white clouds crossing slowly at different depths and speeds, kept very faint so titles and waveforms stay readable. Calmest of the four — it’s weather, not a show.

Side A — Warm Static
Vinyl theme
⏮▶⏭

Vinyl — records turning

Grooved records rotating slowly in the corners — about one turn every 7 seconds, like a real 33 rpm record slowed for ambience — with the amber label and a light shine sweeping as they turn.

Open questions for the real build: background behind the whole player or only the artwork/stage square? Animate always or only while a song plays (kinder to the battery)? And how strong — these are mid-strength; they can go subtler or bolder. Send your examples whenever — nothing gets built until you pick.
