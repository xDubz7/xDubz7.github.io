Stage Preview — visualizers for the artwork square

Five ideas for the big square where the song cover lives, all animated by the same fake 150 BPM mix (intro → build → drop → breakdown → drop, ~32 s loop). A, D and E keep the dancer front and center — the 💃 is a stand-in for your theme's chibi sprite, which in the real player dances on the beat like she does now. B and C replace the artwork outright. Everything here runs on the same precomputed band data the player already makes — no live audio routing, so lock-screen playback stays safe. Colours here are the classic analyzer gradient; a winner gets your theme's colours.

A Dancer Ring — the NCS circle

The iconic NoCopyrightSounds / Trap Nation layout: a circular spectrum pulsing around a center emblem — except the emblem is your dancer, standing in the middle of the ring. Yes, it's a cousin of the strip Radial you just removed; that one was a cramped strip with nothing in the center. This is the stage it actually belongs on. The center disc could hold the song's artwork in a circle instead of the dancer, if you prefer.

B Full-stage spectrum + reflection

The Monstercat / AudioMotion look at full size: big spectrum bars with falling peak caps and a glossy floor reflection. The artwork is gone — the spectrum IS the cover.

C Spectrogram waterfall

The last ~8 seconds of sound as a scrolling heat map — bass at the bottom, treble at the top, brightness is loudness. The DJ-software / sound-engineer view. You can literally see the drop arrive.

D Particle stage

The dancer on a dark stage while particles burst off her on the bass hits and drift up like sparks. The busiest of the dancer options — a little concert, not just a meter.

E Pulse stage — just the dancer

The purest form of your idea: dancer big in the center, nothing competing. A soft glow breathes with the bass and a ring pulses out on the beat. Calm, clean, and the cheapest on battery.

Researched and left out: MilkDrop / Butterchurn (the Winamp psychedelic classic) — it feeds on a live audio analyser, the exact Web Audio routing Localify removed because it killed lock-screen playback; one artist site does drive it from precomputed analysis, but it's a heavy WebGL library expecting hundreds of FFT bins at 60 fps, against our 24 bands at 10 fps. Wrong fit, honestly noted.
