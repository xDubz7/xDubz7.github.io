Wave Style Preview 2 — AudioMotion-inspired

Five styles, all animated by the same fake 150 BPM mix (intro → build → drop → breakdown → drop, ~32 s loop). No audio — just the visuals, the way the first preview worked. In the real player these follow your selected theme's colours; here A/B/C/E use AudioMotion's "classic" gradient and D uses the Localify band colours so you can judge the shapes.

A Spectrum bars + peak caps

AudioMotion's signature look: frequency bars with little caps that hold and slowly fall. In the player it would replace the scrolling strip and needs band data in the sampler — we had exactly that working in v4.7, so it's proven, just moved to the strip.

B LED bars

Same frequency data as A, drawn as chunky stacked LED segments — the club-mixer meter look. Green zone, yellow zone, red zone.

C Line / area curve

Same data again, as a smooth filled curve instead of bars. The calmest of the three spectrum styles.

D Mirror silhouette + reflection

Your current 3-band scrolling strip, mirrored with a faded reflection underneath. Works on the data the player already computes today — the cheapest one to ship, and the only one that shows the song's recent past instead of just this instant.

E Radial spectrum

The spectrum wrapped around a slowly spinning circle. It wants a big square stage — in the player it would live around the artwork, not in the strip under the waveform.
